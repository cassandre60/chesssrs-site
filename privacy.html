Privacy Policy

ChessSRS is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy.

1. The Website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising services. All web fonts and assets are hosted locally on this domain.

The only value stored locally on your device is your preferred visual color theme (light or dark mode) via standard browser localStorage.

2. The Application

The ChessSRS mobile application is a local-first tool. All repertoires, move trees, study chapters, and spaced-repetition schedules are saved directly to a local SQLite database on your device.

Your data is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service unless you explicitly choose to authenticate with Lichess.org to import your own private studies.

3. External Links

This site contains links to external services such as GitHub (for source code and release binaries) and Liberapay (for donations). Those external websites operate under their own independent privacy policies.
