Most trainers ask an engine what is best. ChessSRS asks what you prepared.

Your repertoire is the answer key, including the traps you set for natural opponent mistakes. No engine decides whether you were right.

Reviews run on spaced repetition, so lines you know fade into the background and lines you are shaky on come back sooner.

How it works

Play

Principles

Built to stay out of your way.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ChessSRS free?

Yes. It is free software under GPL-3.0, with no ads and no subscription. If it helps your training you can buy me a coffee to support development.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your device, and reviews work offline once your studies are imported.

How do I add my openings?

Import a PGN file. Multiple chapters and nested variations are supported. To use a Lichess study, export it as PGN first.

How does it decide what is due?

A spaced-repetition scheduler. Lines you remember come back later; lines you miss come back sooner.

Does an engine check my moves?

No. The right answer is the move in your repertoire, even when the opponent's move in the line is inaccurate.

Which platforms does it run on?

ChessSRS is built with Flutter, so one codebase serves several platforms. The releases page lists the builds available for each.

Is it affiliated with Lichess?

No. ChessSRS is an independent fork of Lichess Mobile, released under the same GPL-3.0 licence.
